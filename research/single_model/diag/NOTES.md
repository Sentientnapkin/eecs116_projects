# E1 diagnostics: why bad mid-start flights fail (gp_msckf baseline)

Data: `research/results/single_model/diag_counters1/` (diag build `diag.c`, counters only; ATE identical to baseline: search 2.307 / val 2.032, paired d = +0.000). Per-flight table `diag_table.json`, windows `windows.json`, first-2 s image stats `early_frames.json`. Scripts here: `analyze.py` (parse stderr + truth), `report*.py`. Search slice = folds 0-2 (63 mid, 56 static); val = fold 3 (13 mid, 12 static). Fold 4 never touched. "bad" = ATE > 3 m (search 23/63, val 3/13). Bad flights contribute 3.0 of the 3.76 m search-mid mean (good flights 0.76; val 2.62 of 3.25).

Frame conventions (verified): `ground_truth` in the episode is the simulator world (z up, g = -9.8 z, recovered by least squares from IMU + GT; |g| fit 9.78), NOT body 0. I express everything in the body-0 frame (filter world to body 0 is R0^T from the logged initial R; truth is Rs0^T x). Filter initial up-vector vs true up-vector gives the tilt error.

## 1. Headline

1. The initial conditions do NOT separate bad from good starts. Initial tilt error (filter assumes thrust = gravity): mean 7.0 deg, p50 5.9, p90 13.8, max 24.5 (search mid; true body tilt 6.7 deg). Static starts: 1.3 deg. Initial velocity error = the true start speed (filter starts at 0.02 m/s: the error is all magnitude, 99.7 % of true speed, direction is irrelevant): mean 0.97 m/s, p90 1.9. Good vs bad: tilt0 6.3 vs 7.4 deg (AUC 0.57), v0 0.95 vs 0.99 m/s (AUC 0.52). Bad rate by true tilt: <4 deg 29 %, 4-8 deg 38 %, >8 deg 43 % (weak trend); by start speed 37/33/33/43 % for <0.5/0.5-1/1-1.5/>1.5 m/s (flat). Val agrees (tilt AUC 0.63, v0 AUC 0.17 with n=13, 3 bad).
2. What separates them is whether vision corrects the state during seconds 1-5: features used by the MSCKF in the first 5 s: good 118, bad 35 (AUC 0.08; threshold <= 51: 87 % accuracy search; val AUC 0.13). Attempted features (tracks with >= 3 observations) are the same (52 vs 50 in 2 s), so tracks exist; they are rejected: triangulation failure rate frames 60-100 good 9 % vs bad 48 % (AUC 0.79; val 0.90), chi2-gate rejection of the rest 22-31 % vs 40-70 %.
3. Failure = velocity/attitude runaway plus lock-out. In bad flights the filter's speed estimate at 2 s is 2.0 m/s against 0.94 true, at 5 s 2.3 vs 1.1 (good 0.94 vs 0.8); velocity error at 5 s good 0.36 vs bad 2.16 m/s (AUC 0.97; val 1.00); tilt error at 5 s 1.4 vs 4.3 deg. Velocity std in the filter itself grows: sqrt(trace Pv) at 5 s 0.15 vs 0.53 (AUC 0.95, val 0.93); at 3 s 0.25 vs 0.55 (AUC 0.81). Over-extension (est speed > true) matches the earlier path ratio 1.48.
4. Consistency: tilt NEES at 2 s mean 11 (ideal 2), median 5.9; velocity NEES 40 (ideal 3). Initial tilt sigma 0.05 rad = 2.9 deg vs a mean error of 7 deg (p90 13.8 deg = 4.8 sigma). The 95 % per-feature gate (CHI2_Z 1.645) then throws out good features, which is what keeps the state wrong.

## 2. Error budget at 2 s and 5 s (search mid, mean over flights)

Decomposition (body-0 frame, using truth, the filter's own R(t), b_a(t) and the IMU): position error = (initial velocity error) x T + double integral of tilt/gravity-direction-induced acceleration error + double integral of accel-bias error + corrections (everything vision/prior/thrust-model did, plus noise). Numbers are signed projections on the actual error direction in m (they sum to |error|).

| T, set | |error| | initial velocity | tilt (att + gravity dir) | accel bias | corrections |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 s, all | 1.02 | +0.87 | +0.20 | 0.00 | -0.05 |
| 2 s, all | 1.76 | +1.25 | +1.21 | +0.02 | -0.72 |
| 2 s, good | 1.20 | +1.13 | +0.77 | +0.01 | -0.71 |
| 2 s, bad | 2.73 | +1.46 | +1.97 | +0.03 | -0.73 |
| 5 s, all | 3.02 | +1.86 | +4.27 | +0.16 | -3.27 |
| 5 s, good | 1.19 | +1.86 | +0.39 | +0.07 | -1.14 |
| 5 s, bad | 6.20 | +1.86 | +11.02 | +0.30 | -6.98 |
| val 2 s, all (n=13) | 1.69 | +1.28 | +1.02 | +0.06 | -0.66 |

Ranking of the error sources (positive contributions): at 2 s initial velocity ~51 %, tilt ~49 %, bias ~1 %. Initial velocity is the sure, fixed part (about T x true speed, equal in good and bad). Bias is negligible (accel bias error 0.1-0.15 m/s^2, mean error contribution under 0.2 m at 5 s). The good/bad difference is the tilt term: it is 0.39 m at 5 s in good flights (tilt error shrinks from 6 to 1.4 deg once vision locks) and 11 m in bad flights (tilt error stays 4-6 deg for 5 s). Caveat: the tilt term uses the filter's actual attitude history, so in bad flights it is partly a symptom (attitude is only fixed by vision updates that are being rejected), not purely the initial tilt: initial tilt alone does not predict failure (item 1.1). Direction effect: bad flights have the spurious acceleration from the initial tilt pointing against the start velocity (cos -0.36 search, -0.95 val; good +0.15, -0.04), so the error grows in the same direction as the velocity error; |a_err0| itself AUC 0.58 only. A closed-loop oracle (inject true v0 / true tilt at frame 0, plan item L0) is still needed to give the causal split; this is an open-loop attribution.

## 3. Vision timeline (search mid)

First MSCKF update with accepted features: median frame 18 (0.9 s), p90 30, 4 flights >= frame 40, 1 never. Live tracks first 2 s: 45 mean (p50 53), with >= 3 observations 18. ZUPT never fires on a mid start. The zero-mean velocity prior moves velocity about the same in good and bad flights (cumulative |dv| in 2 s 0.79 vs 0.76 m/s, vision 1.28 vs 1.17): it does not dominate and does not separate the classes. Image statistics: audit flat_fraction AUC 0.63 (search), 0.67 (val), dark_fraction 0.48 (no signal; mean brightness first 2 s: AUC 0.32 on search (bad darker) but 0.77 on val (bad brighter), so not a stable signal); first-2 s flat/dark fractions AUC ~0.55.

## 4. Classification of the failure

Of 23 bad search flights, 4-5 (15 % of the bad-flight ATE excess; val 1 of 3) are starved: live3 in 2 s < 6, flat/dark frames, first update at frame >= 60 or never. That is (b) lock-on / textureless. The other 18 (about 80 % of the excess) have plenty of tracks (live3 12-28), normal first update (frame 8-28) and still fail: initial velocity zero and tilt 7 deg make the first state wrong, vision updates are gated or fail triangulation (state-inconsistent poses give negative or parallel depth), the filter runs away at 2-4 m/s and stays wrong. This is (c): an unrecoverable startup transient of an overconfident estimator, with a self-reinforcing lock-out. Partly (a): the initial priors are the starting point of the transient (tilt sigma too small, velocity prior mean 0 against a 1 m/s truth), but they alone do not decide the outcome (initial tilt/speed overlap completely between classes), so priors help but do not guarantee a fix: classical priors should be expected to cut but not eliminate the 37 % bad rate; a recovery mechanism is needed too. Value check: if all bad flights became the good-class mean (1.2 m), search mid would be 1.2 vs 3.76 now.

## 5. Recommendations

Startup priors (startup/): (1) widen tilt sigma to >= 0.15 rad (p90 error is 0.24 rad) and velocity sigma to >= 1.5 m/s for the first ~2-3 s, conditioned on a motion detector, because the initial state is the only thing both classes share and NEES is 5-30x too high; keep static starts tight (their tilt error is 1.3 deg). (2) The velocity prior is not the problem: its mean is zero and magnitude small compared to vision updates; do not strengthen it, and a non-zero-mean (forward, ~0.9 m/s) prior will remove the 100 %-magnitude error that all starts share (direction is unknown, so use the heading-aware variant only with honest lateral sigma). (3) Account for the tilt-velocity coupling: initial tilt error and velocity error are strongly correlated in a loop (spurious accel opposite to velocity), so an initial cross-covariance of the right sign (or a joint estimate, C4 replay) beats independent inflation.
Health checks (health/): trigger from the filter's own covariance and update statistics, available at 2-3 s: sqrt(trace Pv) > 0.35 m/s at t >= 3 s (good 0.25, bad 0.55 mean, AUC 0.81; at 5 s > 0.25 gives 89 % accuracy, AUC 0.95), triangulation-failure rate of attempted features > 0.4 over frames 40-100, or features used < 7 per second (frames 40-60). Response: soft reset (inflate v and tilt covariance, re-apply startup prior) AND temporarily relax the chi2 gate / allow state to move; both lock-out evidence and the runaway to 2-4 m/s suggest also a speed bound (est speed > 2.2 m/s at 5 s: 83 % accuracy search, 100 % val). Expect the response to act at 3 s, before 5 s error of 6 m (bad flights) accrues.
Textureless (texture/): only ~15-20 % of the bad-flight excess is starvation (live tracks < 6 in 2 s, first update frame >= 60). Fixing it matters for the 5 worst starved flights (ATE 3-10 m) but is not the main lever for mid starts. Lower the detection threshold / brightness-normalized KLT for them and fall back to the strong prior and ZUPT-like logic when tracks < ~6.
General: the difference between 37 % and about 0 % bad starts is ~2.5 m of the 3.76 m search-mid mean, so it dominates every other term (static 0.67). All gating changes must be tested with the trifail / gate-rejection counters from `diag.c` (copy the counters; they add no behaviour). Open-loop attribution here cannot rank initial tilt vs velocity causally: run the oracle (true v0, true tilt, each separately) before investing in a learned startup network.
