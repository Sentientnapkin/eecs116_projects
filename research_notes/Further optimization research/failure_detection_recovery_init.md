# Failure detection, recovery and robust in-flight initialization for a plain-C MSCKF (research notes)

Scope: reduce the rate of catastrophic flights (>3 m ATE, unaligned) of the B7/B11 estimator (monocular 320x240 20 Hz, IMU 200 Hz, 6 clones, GP bias, KLT, startup velocity prior or learned net). Verification labels: [V] = page/abstract/PDF opened and read this session; [S] = search snippet only, not opened; [M] = from memory/unverified. Our own results are labelled by file.

## 0. Bottom line (read this first)
1. The literature has NO published recipe that fixes a wrong-velocity moving start inside a filter. Closed-form / MAP initializers (Martinelli, Kaiser, Campos) need excitation and a window of ~0.5-2 s, and fail or give 5-156% scale error exactly when excitation is low; for constant-velocity mid-air starts, scale is *provably unobservable* for batch VI initializers (Scheiber et al., ICRA21 [V]). Our priors/learned net are the right class of answer; the literature mostly says "add a metric cue (range, depth net, prior)".
2. The literature on in-filter failure handling is thin and mostly reactive: VINS-Mono = reset on few features / pose jump / bias jump [V]; Lee & Johnson = chi2 gate + Student-t re-weighting of repeat outliers [V]; GGIG-MSCKF / RDIC-MSCKF = innovation-adaptive R [S]. Covariance is a weak early-warning signal (Tsuei & Soatto, XIVO: drift only slightly raises uncertainty [S]) -- consistent with our own finding that startup failures are overconfident-wrong, not lock-out.
3. Three cheap in-filter tests derived from this literature were run on B7 (section 5): see results; none is expected to remove the startup-runaway mass. The highest-value untested idea is a *closed-form startup cross-check* (section 3, item 1) that is independent of the heading prior and net.

## 2. What the literature says, by topic
### 2.1 Failure detection
- VINS-Mono rule set (features < N; jump; bias/extrinsic change) is the de-facto standard [V]. In our filter: feature-count rule = the "starved" flag (<25 live tracks, b6fix); jump rule is moot (filter output smooth by construction); bias-change rule: our GP bias states could be monitored (|ba| > 0.5 m/s^2 or |bg| > 0.1 rad/s) -- the health study already tested hard clamps (hurt, health/NOTES.md lim1 +2.96 m) and soft reset (neutral).
- Consistency monitors: NIS windows on the visual update, innovation whiteness, NEES (needs truth: use only offline). In MSCKF, the per-feature chi2 gate *is* a NIS test. Practical signals: accepted-feature fraction (our b6fix finding: bad flights 1.0 vs 1.9 accepted/frame), mean m2/k0 (should be 1), rejection rate in first 2 s.
- Information-matrix eigenvalues with hysteresis (2611.07687 [V]) = degeneracy detection; in a 6-clone MSCKF the visual information matrix is a by-product of the stacked H (after QR compress): compute min eigenvalue of H^T H restricted to the velocity/yaw/tilt subspace. Cost O(18^3) once per frame; no published evidence for monocular indoor flight.
- Learned predictors: none found at failure-prediction level; only learned uncertainty/weights (2609.39125, 2510.01648, 2607.17956).
### 2.2 Recovery
- Reset-and-reinitialize (VINS-Mono) breaks trajectory continuity; ORB-SLAM3 Atlas needs maps; Faessler needs actuation. For an unaligned ATE metric a *re-anchor* (re-initialize velocity/tilt/bias state while keeping pose continuous) is the only compatible form; our multihyp result (late switching cannot undo an early offset) says it helps only for late failures (starved coasting, onset >= 6 s), not startup runaway.
- Robust update: chi2 gate + Student-t (Lee & Johnson [V]), Huber/GGIG/Sage-Husa [S]. Our history: Huber beyond the gate (INV_HUBER) rejected (inventive/NOTES.md row o: search 1.375 vs 1.028); adaptive gate under starvation A3 rejected (b6fix).
- Coasting under starvation: LBDU-VIO [V] learned bias dynamics + adaptive IMU noise is the only recent in-MSCKF evidence (-25% under 10 s outage). Our "starvation mode" already helped alone (health).
### 2.3 Initialization for moving starts
- Closed-form/MAP initializers solve v0, g, scale (+ba) jointly: observability needs acceleration change and parallax; scale error 5-6% typical in good excitation, up to 156% (Campos 2019 [V]) in bad cases; with constant velocity, scale unobservable [V]. For us scale errors are fatal for unaligned ATE.
- Fast variants (0.3-0.5 s, learned depth) exist [V] but need a depth net (too heavy for C wasm budget).
- Filters (OpenVINS dynamic init [S], sqrtVINS [V]) solve a linear system for v0, g from a window then refine with an iterative update.
- Our setting differs favourably: gravity direction is nearly known (thrust-aligned specific force is NOT gravity in motion, but tilt is bounded <35 deg per health/NOTES C6 table), speed prior known (p50 0.81, p99 2.77 m/s), and IMU scale is metric. It differs unfavourably: 0.5-2 s windows at ~1 m/s with weak accelerations (low parallax at ~2-4 m depth, 122 deg FOV).
### 2.4 Containment / planner priors
- No literature found specific to MSCKF with room-extent priors; the closest practice is map-based soft constraints. Our own evidence: hard plausibility clamps hurt (health lim1/lim2tight: +2.96/+2.83 m search); C6 envelope priors (soft) gave -0.6 m on the old baseline, mostly captured by the shipped G build. The tailcap agent covers this; do not duplicate.

## 3. Ranked techniques (with C implementation sketch, expected tail effect, cheap falsification)
Ranking = (expected reduction of the >3 m mass on B7/B11) x (probability the evidence transfers) / cost. Expected effects are my estimates, not measurements, unless stated.

1. Closed-form startup velocity cross-check (Kaiser/Campos-lite, linear, no depth net). NOT TESTED (needs >1 day to do properly; B11's net already covers the same slot; test on B7 only as a feature to the net or as a validator).
   - Build: first 10 frames (0.5 s), known gyro-integrated rotations R_i (error small over 0.5 s), unknowns v0 (3), gravity tilt correction (2, since |g|=9.81 and f direction ~ thrust), per-feature depth eliminated by cross-product constraints [b_ij]x (R_ij (lambda_i0 b_i0 + ... ) ) = 0 -> linear least squares over (v0, g, scaled landmark) with N~20 features -> 5 unknowns + N depths, solve by normal equations / Cholesky (existing cholesky()). Use the double-integrated specific force (rotated by R_i) as the known displacement term. Validate with the condition number / smallest singular value (Campos observability test) and a RANSAC over feature subsets (Merrill [V]).
   - Use: not as the prior mean but as a *veto*: if the cross-check disagrees with the prior/net velocity by > k sigma and the solve is well-conditioned, widen the startup velocity sd (or flip to the reverse hypothesis). Expected effect on tails: addresses wrong-direction (6/21 fresh >3 m flights) and fast (3/21); expected -20..-35% of those if the linear solve is accurate to ~0.3 m/s at 0.5 s (uncertain: low parallax). Falsification: offline in Python on truth+tracks of the 34 mid >135 deg flights and the 33 regression flights: if the solved v0 direction error is >60 deg on >50% of them, kill it (cheap: no C).
2. Two-step gating (re-test rejected features against the posterior). TESTED (section 5).
   - Implementation: split msckf_update into core + wrapper; keep the batch entries rejected by the chi2 gate; after the first update re-triangulate with the corrected clones and re-gate; second update on the survivors. ~25 lines; fuel cost up on rejected features only.
   - Rationale: a state error larger than sigma causes gate rejections of *good* features, the lock-out mechanism; the posterior from the accepted subset is closer so the rest can pass. Literature support is indirect (Lee & Johnson [V] re-weight failing features instead of dropping them).
3. Covariance matching (Sage-Husa-like) pixel-noise scale. TESTED. s += rate*s*(clamp(m2/k0)-1), s in [1,4]: inflates R when innovations are persistently too large (equivalent to adaptive gate + weaker corrections).
4. In-gate Huber/Student-t weights (M-estimator). TESTED. weight = (lim/m2)^(1/4) on (H,r) rows when sqrt(m2) > LIT_HUB*sqrt(k0).
5. Early-warning monitor + re-anchor (NIS/accepted-fraction over first 2 s, then re-initialize velocity from the net with pose continuity). Not tested: our own b6fix A3/A5 arms (gate widening and covariance inflation in lock-out) were neutral and multihyp says late switching fails; only worth it as a *fast* (<1 s) trigger. Expected effect small, <=10% of tail mass.
6. Scale-trust from excitation (2603.26740 [V]): compute curvature-acceleration energy over the window; when low (constant-velocity-like), inflate scale-related velocity sd / disable vision-driven velocity updates that would lock a wrong scale. Cheap: one scalar from accel in R-frame (a - mean). Falsification: correlate that scalar with final-ATE > 3 m on the 171 flights; kill if AUC < 0.6. Expected effect on fast/slow-start tails 10-20% if informative.
7. Information-eigenvalue degeneracy monitor with hysteresis (2611.07687 [V]); diagnostic only for starved coasting; response = ramp-down of velocity update strength or switch to coasting prior. Low evidence.
8. Learned adaptive IMU noise/bias dynamics for coasting (LBDU-VIO [V], AirIMU [V]): -25% under outage on EuRoC; heavy to port (neural ODE). Expected effect on starved-coasting tails (9/21 fresh >3 m) maybe -15%; the cheapest analogue is scheduling GP bias process noise with flight state (no net). Overlaps with other agents (learned components).
9. Not recommended here: Faessler active recovery, ORB-SLAM3 Atlas/relocalization, laser-range init, depth-net init (no hardware/size budget), hard plausibility clamps (negative evidence), FEJ/OC (negative evidence).
