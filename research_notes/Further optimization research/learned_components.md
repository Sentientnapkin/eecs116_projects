# Small learned components for a classical VIO filter (C/wasm) - literature + 2 falsification tests
Date 2026-11-20. Code/results: research/single_model/litlearn/ (prep_imu.py, train_imuvel.py, cmp_filter.py, fuse.py, eval_honest.py, monitor.py, logs, imuvel_*.pt/json).
Rules kept: no holdout, no fold 4, no sealed json, no private episodes, no filter evals run (tests are offline on saved B11 r0 estimate.tum of the 107 unselected fold-3 flights; nets trained on the 475 folds-0-2 raw-fast flights).
Citation labels: [V] = arXiv ID verified via arXiv API metadata (title/date) AND abstract read; [V-id] = ID/title verified, numbers from search snippet or fetched HTML only; [mem] = from memory, unverified.

## 0. Key context from the simulator-structure study (simulator_structure.md) - what a hand-written prior already captures
Dynamics are drag-free and thrust-along-body-z: specific force in body axes = (0,0,|a-g|) exactly. Consequences for every IMU-learned idea:
* The accelerometer carries NO tilt and NO velocity information; a net can only get horizontal acceleration from the ATTITUDE (tilt = atan(a_h/g)), i.e. from the filter's own attitude. Learned IMU-window velocity nets (TLIO/AirIO/AI-IO style) therefore do not add independent inertial information here; what they add is the learned KINEMATIC prior: heading chases velocity (v_y = 0.99 w_z |v_h|, R2 0.84), speed envelope, bang-bang yaw rate, start tilt = -a_h/g. AirIO's 'body frame + attitude' gain is the same thing (they note rotor/thrust cues; AI-IO adds rotor speed which we do not have).
* Hand-written physical priors that already cover learned components: coordinated-turn lateral-velocity pseudo-measurement (covers most of the learnable v_y), anisotropic start-tilt prior, static-start ZUPT, flatness tilt-from-acceleration, bounded accel envelope. A learned net is only worth it for what these leave: forward speed (v_x R2 0.63 with w_z, sat, w_y; learnable remainder), regime/uncertainty weighting, and visual cues (derotated flow).

## 2. Falsification tests (offline, existing flights)
### Test A: IMU-window displacement net vs the filter's own 1-4 s displacement error (fold-3, 107 flights)
Setup: TLIO-style 1D-CNN (237 K params; 4 stride-2 conv blocks 6/9ch-> 32/64/96/128 + FC; body-frame IMU 200 Hz + gravity direction in body, output body-frame displacement + log-var), trained 8 k steps (~1 min GPU) on 475 folds-0-2 flights, tested on 107 unselected fold-3 flights vs B11 r0 estimate.tum (rotation-invariant |window displacement error|).
Results (rms of window displacement error, m):
* Net with TRUTH gravity direction (optimistic): 1 s window net 0.15 (filter 0.215; good flights ATE<1: 0.154 vs 0.138; filter 0.55 / net 0.15 on 1-3 m flights, 0.79 vs 0.16 on the >3 m flight). 2 s: net 0.204 vs filter 0.340 (median 0.119 vs 0.129). 4 s: 0.288. 0.5 s: 0.109 (per-time error falls with window length: 0.22/0.15/0.10/0.07 m/s for 0.5/1/2/4 s). Variant A (truth yaw-aligned frame) 0.179 vs B 0.152.
* TILT SENSITIVITY (consequence of Section 0): adding per-window gravity-direction noise of 1 deg raises the 2 s error 0.20 -> 0.93 m; 3 deg -> 1.53 m. 1 deg tilt = 0.17 m/s^2 = 0.34 m over 2 s. The truth-attitude numbers above are therefore NOT achievable by an honest filter.
* HONEST variant (gravity-in-body from the FILTER's attitude, gravity axis from the first 20 s of filter-rotated specific force; resulting tilt error 0.86 deg rms, like the filter's 0.76): net trained without tilt noise 0.360 vs filter 0.359 (no gain; fused w=0.8: 0.526 vs 0.543). Net trained with tilt-noise augmentation (0.5 / 1.0 deg): 0.292 / 0.293 vs filter 0.359.
* Post-hoc fusion (2 s non-overlapping windows, sampled ATE; filter 0.543; increments w*filter+(1-w)*net, net rotated with the filter's own attitude): honest tilt-aug net, w=0.8: 0.499 / 0.493 (-8 to -9 %, better on 60/107 flights, median 0.364 -> 0.342-0.353); gated (disagreement >1.0 m over 2 s -> use net only) 0.453 / 0.451 (-17 %, better on 54-58/107). Pure net dead reckoning 0.91-0.94 (no vision!), 0 flights >3 m, filter 1. (With truth attitude, 2 s: w=0.8 0.480, gated 0.443; 4 s window w=0.8 0.471.)
Caveats: w, threshold and tilt-aug level were picked on the same 107 flights (2-3 knobs; small optimism); the constant-weight fusion is crude (a covariance-weighted in-filter update should do at least as well but errors of net and filter are not independent: both rely on the same attitude); net trained on folds 0-2 scenes but fold 3 is scene-disjoint; one seed; sim-only.
Verdict: NOT falsified, but small and mostly a learned kinematic prior. Expected in-filter effect on mean ATE -5..-15 % (point -9 %), mostly on the 0.5-3 m flights, median -5 %; tail rescue limited because a >3 m flight in these sets is mostly startup failure (needs the net before the divergence, i.e. a 2 s lag) - the gated variant is the only part that touches the tail. An in-filter test IS warranted but only as a cheap one: 2 s displacement/velocity measurement (every 10-20 frames, sd from net log-var x calibrated 1.2), tilt-augmented net, cost ~4.6 M MAC per call (see C cost). Check first that the hand-written coordinated-turn prior does not give the same gain (it captures lateral part; the net also learns speed regime and visual-free forward speed).

### Test B: net-vs-filter disagreement as an early-warning monitor (same nets)
Disagreement = |filter 2 s displacement - net 2 s displacement| (honest tilt-aug net, filter attitude). 107 f3 flights, 9 with ATE>1 m (1-2 with >3 m: this set has too few tail flights for a >3 m statement).
AUC for ATE>1 from mean disagreement in the first 6/10/20 s: 0.81/0.87/0.95 (truth-gravity net: 0.81/0.88/0.96). Threshold 1.0 m: flags 9/9 bad flights (detection time 2-12 s for 8/9, errors at detection 1.1-2.2 m), 11/98 false alarms; threshold 1.5 m: 8/9 flagged, 0/98 false alarms, detection 2-8 s (one at 42 s), error at detection 1.25-3.9 m. So the monitor sees failures at about the time the error reaches 1.5-2 m, i.e. about 3-6 s into a startup divergence: usable to trigger a re-anchor/re-init (the failure-detection note's recovery path), not as a predictor before the divergence. Caveat: monitor and gate saw the same flights for threshold choice.

## 3. Ranked components (a = tail effect, b = median effect; est. on mean unaligned ATE; 'prior' = what a hand-written physical prior already captures)
1. Learned 2 s displacement/velocity measurement (tilt-augmented body-frame IMU net, Test A) + disagreement gating/monitor (Test B). Tail: modest (-0 to -15 %, mainly via gate+re-anchor), median -5 %. Prior overlap: HIGH for the lateral part (v_y = 0.99 w_z |v_h|, tilt = -a_h/g); do the hand-coded coordinated-turn pseudo-measurement first, then train the net on the RESIDUAL (inputs including the physical-model prediction) so it only learns forward speed and regime/sigma. Design: 237 K params, 4.6 M MAC at 2 s window (halve by 100 Hz average pooling and width 24/48/64/96: ~1.3 M MAC), float32 0.95 MB (0.25 MB narrow), fuel ~2.8 fuel/MAC (measured from the startup net) -> 13 M per call at full size: must be spread over >=4 frames or use the narrow net (~3.6 M); run every 10-20 frames. Integration: displacement/velocity pseudo-measurement with attitude-propagated rotation, sd = 1.2 x net sigma, robust gate. Falsified by: in-filter 3-rep f3 gain <0.03.
2. Larger/more diverse startup-net training data + tilt/speed augmentation (startup net data scaling; net-to-net spread +-0.05 and only 475 flights): generate 1.5-3 k extra flights with research/gen.py (GPUs idle), include the server-like path length (~60 m) and speeds >1.5 m/s (only 10 % of anchors, error 0.40 m/s there, cov1 0.56). Tail: highest (12/21 of >3 m flights are startup); median ~0. Prior overlap: partial (heading-prior/ coordinated-turn tell the lateral part). Compute at inference unchanged. Owner of the startup net is another agent: this is a recommendation only.
3. Prior-informed startup net (feed the hand-written physical predictions v_y = 0.99 w_z|v_h|, tilt = -a_h/g, regime flags as input channels or targets residuals; learn only the remaining forward speed/sign ambiguity). Tail: strongest for wrong-direction starts (6/21 >3 m), because the hard part (forward/backward ambiguity when heading is >90 deg off the velocity) is exactly where the prior is weakest (26 % of saturated-turn starts are backward/>90 deg). Median ~0. Free at inference (a few MACs). Prior overlap: this IS the combination.
4. Variance calibration of the net output (VeloBins-style error-conditioned label or temperature scaling per speed bin; cov1 0.56 at >1.5 m/s, global optimal scale 1.16 single net): cheap, 0 extra C cost (a 5-entry table). Tail: small positive (over-confident fast starts); median 0. Not statistically testable without in-filter replicates (+-0.05 chaos); B12 'sigma floor' variants were mixed.
5. Learned risk/failure monitor (Test B) feeding re-initialisation: AUC 0.87-0.95 on 107 flights. Value = only if a recovery action exists (re-anchor with the startup net velocity); the b6fix numbers say starved coasting (9/21) and startup (12/21): the monitor catches both as filter-vs-IMU-kinematics disagreement. Tail: potentially large (the only candidate that targets >3 m flights directly), median 0. Risk: false alarms (11/98 at 1.0 m) must be handled by a soft re-weighting rather than a hard reset. Needs fuel for one extra net call only when triggered.
6. Learned feature-track quality (tiny MLP on patch stats, track age, parallax) replacing chi2 gating: no literature precedent found; b6fix A3/A5 (relaxed gate, inflation) gave <= 0.003 m, FB gating costs 27 % of long tracks. Expect <= 0.02 m median; tail unclear (starved coasting 9/21 is track-count not gating). Cheap in C (<50 K MAC per frame) but needs per-track labels from the filter residuals (self-labelled from truth reprojection), so effort is high for a low expected gain.
7. Learned (adaptive) measurement noise R / process noise Q heads (2203.07207, VIO-DualProNet snippet): net-predicted per-frame pixel sd from image statistics (blur, exposure). Median -2..-4 %, tail 0. The OpenVINS-tricks note found separate 1.0 px noise +0.091 worse, so noise tuning is already near a flat optimum.
8. Monocular depth/inverse-depth prior for low-parallax triangulation (2204.09171, Merrill RSS23 snippet, MDE-VIO 2602.11323): needs a depth net at 128x96 (a student of Depth Anything V2 2406.09414 [V-id] distilled offline would be ~0.3-1 M params, 20+ M MACs = ~60 M fuel per frame: far over budget unless run on 1 frame in 10). Could shorten the 3-10 s startup offset (15 % of median ATE) but the bulk-error note says drift is horizontal wander; skeptical, low priority.
Not recommended: learned IMU bias/gravity/tilt (accelerometer carries zero tilt info here; biases are observable and the filter tilt is 0.76 deg; [V] LBMSCKF gains are on real data with aerodynamics), test-time adaptation (needs backprop in the guest; RIO/UL-VIO [V-id] are not C-friendly), heavy distillation of DPVO/MASt3R/VGGT into a student velocity net (no precedent; our own pseudo-labels from truth are better and free), ensembles (B12: -7 % direct error, no in-filter gain), pruning/quantisation (only needed if comps 1/5 are enlarged: int8 conv is plain C, 4x smaller; fuel is MAC-bound so no fuel gain without SIMD).
Teacher-based pseudo-labels note: truth is available for all simulated flights, so a teacher only helps for real-world data; not for this sim-only benchmark.

## 4. Data and training notes
Test A used no new data (475 train / 107 f3 flights, 1 min per net on one GPU). Training on windows at every IMU sample end, flights are 60 s: 285 k windows per epoch-equivalent. Tilt-aug (random per-window tilt noise up to 2x TGN deg, applied to the gravity channels) is essential: without it the net is a tilt-integrator that doubles its error at 0.86 deg real tilt noise. Next data step if comps 1/3 proceed: server-like 60 m flights, speeds up to 2 m/s, more start phases.
