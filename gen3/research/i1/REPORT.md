## 1. Exact scoring
- EXISTING: implementation is viobench-provided-openvins/viobench/tools/server/evaluations.py (`dominated_area`, `area_gains`, `comparison_for`). Points are (fuel_per_frame_max over all frames of all 24 episodes, mean ATE over 24 episodes), both clamped to floors (fuel 1, ATE 0.001). Area is the staircase region dominated by the Pareto set in ln-ln coordinates, bounded by fuel_reference = 1.2 x step_fuel = 2.4e10 and error_reference = 1.2 x ATE of the LEFTMOST (lowest-fuel) baseline.
- EXISTING (important): a submission's gain = dominated_area(baselines + that one point) - dominated_area(baselines). `claimed_area` = the gain of the single best submission (`best_submission` = argmax gain). Gains are computed independently per submission against the BASELINE set only (is_baseline flag), never against our other submissions or other teams' non-baseline points.
  - MATH consequence: only ONE point per team counts. Complementary frontier points give no extra credit; a second submission is worth nothing unless it beats the first in gain. One improved solution > several complementary ones. (Inference from the code of this local copy of the server tool; whether the course leaderboard uses the same per-team rule is UNKNOWN, but comparison.json `claimed_area` matches.)
  - Overlap: points inside regions already dominated by baselines earn 0 for that part; a point with y>=current boundary adds nothing (code line `if y >= by: continue`).

Rectangle = 221.43 ln-units^2 (fuel_reference 2.4e10, error_reference 10.554 m = 1.2 x 8.795 m of "Run 11..." baseline, comparison.json). ## 3. Baseline fuel breakdown (A160+Ze0) - MEASURED
Method (NEW EXPERIMENT): instrumented build builds/i1_prof (host env.mark(id) at segment boundaries via mkprof.py; fuel between marks charged to the later mark), run on all 358 v3val flights x 1200 frames (429,600 frames). Instrumented peak equals uninstrumented peak on every flight (ratio 1.0001, n=358), so the breakdown is faithful. Details in prof_summary.md.
- Per-frame: max 3,738,591; p99 3.25 M; p95 2.82 M; median 2.27 M; mean 2.10 M. Per-flight peak median 3.47 M, p90 3.63 M. Frames >=3 M: 3.06 %; >=3.6 M: 0.02 %. Peak frame is never the init frame (init frame itself 2.0 M).
- Share of ALL fuel: EKF visual update 28.5 %, KLT tracking 15.7 %, triangulation/Jacobians/nullspace (build_rows) 13.0 %, image->gray+pyramid 10.0 % (211 k per frame, constant), IMU measurements 5.8 % (123 k/frame), feature detection 5.1 %, net inference micro-steps total ~17 % (k<16 1.6, k16-31 1.6, k32-55 2.6, k56-61 1.4, k62-73 2.5, k74-85 3.8, k86-91 1.7, k92-94 0.7, head 1.0), map relocalize 1.1, net re-anchor/startup 1.0, IMU filter+prop+cov ~1.7, rest <1.
- Share on per-flight PEAK frames (what the score sees): net micro-steps k74-85 40.9 %, k86-91 20.5 %, KLT 9.4 %, gray+pyramid 6.1 %, detection 4.3 %, net head 4.4 %, k92-94 4.0 %, IMU measurements 3.6 %, map relocalize 3.5 %. Update/build_rows are ~0 % of peak frames: the governor keeps MSCKF rows below the peak, the peak is set by the net's conv layers (large single micro-step units up to 1.87 M) plus a fixed ~0.7 M of per-frame vision (pyramid+KLT+IMU).
- Peak if a component were free (MEASURED): net k74-85: 3.28 M (-12 %); gray+pyramid: 3.53 M (-5.6 %); map relocalize 3.60; KLT 3.61; IMU measurements 3.62; net k86-91 3.57; detection 3.67. Others <0.5 %.
- Interpretation (MATH/HYPOTHESIS): peak is a governed design quantity (GOV_B ~3.6 M budget), not a natural maximum; 2.27 M median is mostly a fixed floor of ~0.7 M vision + update fill. Cutting the peak substantially (e.g. to 1-2 M) requires splitting the largest net micro-steps (k74-91 layers, up to 1.87 M each) and capping KLT/detect bursts, AND lowering the MSCKF row cap; each costs accuracy (per earlier results small nets help only at <=3 M). Governor overshoot: global peak 3.74 M vs budget ~3.6 M = ~4 % overshoot (MEASURED difference; budget value EXISTING from source `3.6e6`).
- Not separately measured (UNKNOWN): recovery paths, "init" beyond the first frame (init frame is 2.0 M and below peak).

## 4. Fuel of model sizes - MEASURED (bench.tsv)
3x3 valid-conv stacks compiled with the competition toolchain (wasi-sdk clang -O3 -msimd128), run under viobench's engine config; fuel is for one full forward pass of the stack. Three kernels: f32 (f32x4), i16 (dot_i16x8), i8r (relaxed i8 dot-add).
- Fuel per MAC: f32 3.08-3.13; i16 1.47 (tiny 14 M-MAC stack) to 2.0-2.3 (larger); i8r 0.74-0.93. So int16 ~1.5-2x cheaper than f32, int8 relaxed ~3.5-4x cheaper than f32 (accuracy of int8 nets NOT tested).
- Measured totals per forward: f32: 14 M MACs = 43.7 M fuel; 27 M MACs = 83.6 M; 145 M MACs = 446 M; 306 M MACs = 940 M. i16: 14 M MACs = 20.6 M; 43 M MACs = 94.9 M; 201 M MACs = 408 M; 403 M MACs = 839 M. i8r: 14 M MACs = 12.9 M; 27 M = 24.1 M; 94 M = 85 M; 526 M = 402 M; 1143 M MACs = 840 M.
- Row targets 1/5/10/25 M collapse to the smallest stack (13-44 M) in bench.tsv: my smallest benchmark network already exceeds 10 M in one pass (14 M MACs). Reaching a 1-5 M forward needs < ~1-3 M MACs (i8r/i16). HYPOTHESIS by linear scaling of the measured fuel/MAC; not measured at that size. The deployed baseline net amortises its ~8.7 M total (EXISTING) over frames as micro-steps so peak stays ~3.7 M.
- Peak implication (MATH): a net run in one step costs its full forward; to hold a 1 M peak the net must be sliced across frames (already done) - slicing is free in fuel terms, so net capacity is limited by total per-window budget not by peak. Models at 100-1000 M fuel are feasible only for Target C/D regimes (peak 1e8-1e9) as a single step, or sliced over ~25-250 frames at 4 M/frame.
