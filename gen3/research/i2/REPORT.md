## 0. Method and validity
- NEW EXPERIMENT: instrumented copy src_i2.c (= src_base.c + a `#if I2_DBG` fprintf of frame, R, vel, pos, bg, ba, diag(P) of 15 core states, track counts, per frame) built to builds/dbg (sha 98c632ae; the repro build builds/repro reproduces 670bc1da exactly). Eval i2_dbg on v3val: per-flight ATEs are BIT-IDENTICAL (max |diff| 2e-16) to sw_a1_Ze0 run 0, so the instrumentation changes nothing. MEASURED.
- Frames: GT/estimates are in the camera-0 frame ("c0"), where c0 z is camera-forward, NOT up. Vertical = component along `up` (true gravity-up in c0, from GT). (An early pass that used z by mistake gave 45% vertical share; fixed, numbers below use the up projection.) Horizontal = remainder.
- Biases: "truth" is an offline LS estimate from GT+IMU in 5 s windows (gyro mean(gyro - GT rate), accel by 1 s velocity-increment fit); error floors ~ LS accuracy, so bias-error numbers are indicative. Tilt error = angle between the filter's gravity-in-body and GT's.
- Single-run caveat: chaos spread per flight is large; population statistics (n=358) are robust, per-flight claims are not.

Findings:
- Growth law (MEASURED): |pos err| median 0.19 (2 s) -> 0.27 (5) -> 0.33 (10) -> 0.40 (20) -> 0.57 (40) -> 0.67 (60 s). Log-log slope for t>2 s per flight: median 0.35, IQR [0.12, 0.58] (plots/growth_scale.png): SUB-LINEAR, roughly t^0.35-0.5, between a random walk (0.5) and bounded. Not quadratic, not linear drift. A fast early rise (0.1 -> 0.37 m by 2 s) plus a slow ~0.2 m/10 s... drift thereafter (about 1% of path length at the end: 0.83 / ~75 m).
- Gravity/tilt: 5.5 deg at 0.25 s, 2.9 at 1 s, 1.26 at 2 s, 0.5 deg floor from 10 s (floor is likely partly my accel-LS/gravity truth noise; HYPOTHESIS). Velocity: 0.86 -> 0.33 (2 s) -> 0.14 m/s plateau after 10 s; a plateau of 0.13-0.15 m/s over a 1.27 m/s mean speed (~11%) is the standing velocity error of the filter+net re-anchor.
- Yaw error grows monotonically ~ sqrt(t)-ish: 0.7 deg (2 s), 1.6 (10 s), 2.9 (40 s), 3.4 (60 s) (absolute, in c0-measured axes). At ~1.27 m/s and 75 m path, 3.4 deg yaw gives up to ~2.2 m lateral for a straight line if it were all heading error; the realised error (0.77 horizontal at the end) means heading drift is a plausible large contributor (HYPOTHESIS, needs i3 oracle; r10 post-hoc best-yaw-about-origin gain was only 0.655->0.633 so a constant initial yaw offset is NOT it, i.e. the yaw drift is progressive, EXISTING r10 + MEASURED).
- Bias: accel-bias error falls 0.11 -> 0.012 m/s2 by 20 s, gyro 0.020 -> 0.004 rad/s (these are at the LS-truth noise floor late). Late accel bias error 0.012 m/s2 over 60 s integrates to 0.5*0.012*60^2 = 21 m if uncorrected (MATH), so velocity/vision corrections are doing the real work; bias is NOT the limiting error late (HYPOTHESIS: consistent with earlier "bias ~1%" in single_model/diag, EXISTING).
- Velocity-error autocorrelation (MEASURED, mean over flights, lags in frames at ~20 Hz): 0.81 (5), 0.62 (10), 0.44 (20), 0.18 (40), ~0 (80+). Correlation time ~1-2 s: velocity errors are slowly varying (colored) with ~1.5 s correlation, not white and not a persistent bias; consistent with the re-anchor period (10 frames) giving partially independent net errors plus filter memory.
- Local accuracy vs global offset (MEASURED): RPE over 1 s mean 0.21 m (median relative error 12.1% of the GT displacement), over 5 s 0.43 m (median relative 6.9%). ATE 0.655. ATE correlates with RPE5 at rho 0.83. Best constant offset (r10, non-causal) 0.445; best linear 0.348; best quadratic 0.300 (EXISTING r10 ph_base.json, re-checked): i.e. ATE falls 0.655 -> 0.445 with a constant offset, -> 0.30 with a quadratic (initial position + velocity + acceleration-like corrections), so ~55% of ATE is a smooth low-order component. The RPE5 of 0.43 m shows that the error is also not just a smooth offset: the 1-5 s relative motion itself is off by 7-12%.
- Scale (MEASURED): LS scale of estimated vs GT path per flight: median 0.993 IQR [0.973, 1.011]: scale is unbiased; ratio of estimated to GT path length median 1.08 IQR [1.054, 1.111] (extra length = jitter, HYPOTHESIS). Scale error is NOT a systematic problem (rho of ATE with LS scale -0.10).
- Failure concentration (MEASURED): ATE percentiles 50/90/99 = 0.60 / 1.13 / 2.01; no flight >3 m. Worst 10% flights hold 23.5% of sum ATE (42.5% of sum ATE^2); worst 25% hold 46%. Broad drift, not a tail.
- Share of final error from the start (MEASURED + MATH): mean |pos err| at 1 s / 2 s / 5 s = 0.235 / 0.366 / 0.367 m versus mean ATE 0.655, i.e. the 2 s position error equals 56% of ATE in magnitude, BUT it is not a persistent offset: see next item.
- Verification of "post-hoc start-error removal doesn't help" (r10 posthoc.py output, re-checked here on 358 baseline flights and replicates; EXISTING tool, MEASURED numbers): subtracting e(T) from all t>=T: ATE 0.655 -> 0.652 (T=0.5), 0.679 (1 s), 0.740 (2 s), 0.679 (3 s), 0.660 (5 s); the replicates r1/r2 and v1val agree (v1val 0.556 -> 0.602 at 1 s). ## 3. Q7: vertical (plots/horiz_vs_vert.png)
- True vertical motion (MEASURED, up = GT gravity direction): GT vertical std mean 1.22 m (median 1.54), range mean 4.3 m, vertical speed RMS 0.59 m/s versus mean speed 1.27 m/s. By scene vertical std: rooms 0.38 m, warehouse 1.80, forest 1.73. Flights are NOT constant-altitude; there is substantial vertical motion outdoors/warehouse.
- Error split (MEASURED): mean RMS horizontal error 0.609 m, vertical 0.208 m. Vertical share of squared ATE = 11.4%; median ate_v/ate_h = 0.32. If vertical error were zeroed ATE would be 0.609 vs 0.655 (MATH on existing errors: removing the vertical component of the error vector, not a re-run; real value of a vertical constraint is capped at ~7% ATE, ~0.05 m, ~+0.1 score by the BRIEF rule; i3 oracle runs needed for actual). Vertical error is therefore NOT the bottleneck; horizontal (incl. heading-driven) error is.
- Vertical error type (MEASURED): LS fit of vertical error to offset + linear drift: mean |offset| 0.109 m, mean |drift| 0.004 m/s (0.24 m per 60 s), residual RMS 0.113 m. So vertical error is a mixture of offset (~0.11) and slowly varying wander (~0.11), with small linear drift. Estimated vertical motion amplitude vs true: LS gain median 0.99 (IQR 0.95-1.04): vertical motion is NOT under-estimated (no collapse of vertical motion).
- ATE_v by scene: room 0.148, warehouse 0.232, forest 0.267; by true vertical-std tercile: 0.149 / 0.230 / 0.243; Spearman with GT vertical std +0.46, with vertical speed RMS +0.46: vertical error scales with how much the drone moves vertically (consistent with gravity-direction/scale effects), not with a constant offset.
- Correlations (MEASURED): ate_v vs tilt error (t>2 s) rho +0.21, vs tilt at 5 s +0.07, vs |accel-bias error| (t>2 s) +0.27, vs GT initial vertical speed +0.11, vs early (1 s) position error +0.23, with final signed vertical error +0.72 (of course). Weak-to-moderate: tilt/bias explain little; vertical error tracks vertical travel.
- False vertical motion in level flight: rooms (GT vertical std 0.38 m) show vertical RMS error 0.148 m; flights with GT vertical std <1.0 m (n=143): ate_h 0.436, ate_v 0.148 (stats.txt line 'flat flights' now uses <1.0 m). No separate analysis of strictly constant-altitude flights (UNKNOWN: n with std <0.1 m is 0 in v3val).
- Floor/ceiling visibility, plane/height constraint value: UNKNOWN here (no scene metadata labels analysed; no depth available per BRIEF; oracle runs belong to i3). HYPOTHESIS: a height prior gains <= ~0.05 m ATE given the 11% vertical share.

## 5. Direct answers / implications (labels in brackets)
1. Error is broad, sub-linear (~t^0.35-0.5) drift after a fast initial rise; not a tail (worst 10% = 23% of total) [MEASURED].
2. The start is NOT a persistent offset: removing the 2 s position error post hoc makes ATE worse (0.740); start-state errors correlate weakly with ATE (rho 0.05-0.34) [MEASURED, r10 confirmed]. Real start benefits need i3's oracle injection (true v0/gravity), unresolved [UNKNOWN].
3. Local accuracy: RPE1 0.21 m (12% of travel), RPE5 0.43 m (7%); ATE tracks RPE5 (rho 0.83); a smooth quadratic would remove ~55% of ATE (0.655->0.30) - i.e. most error is low-order/smooth (offset, velocity, slow drift, heading) [MEASURED/EXISTING r10]. Scale unbiased (0.993) [MEASURED].
4. Heading/yaw drift (3.4 deg at 60 s, monotone) plus horizontal velocity plateau (0.14 m/s, 11% of speed) are the likely late-error drivers; gravity/bias are small late [MEASURED states; causal attribution HYPOTHESIS].
5. Vertical is 11% of squared ATE; vertical motion is tracked with gain 0.99 [MEASURED]. De-prioritise vertical constraints.
6. Warehouse (0.83) and forest (0.72) rise with path (long, far scenes: weak parallax; mean live tracks saturated at ~59/60 yet worse than rooms) [MEASURED; mechanism HYPOTHESIS]; rooms short with strong rotation (best).
7. Suggested next (for i3, not done): inject true heading/yaw-rate and horizontal velocity periodically vs throughout, to separate velocity plateau vs heading contribution.
