Reading (physics vs prior):
- IMU-only / single image / orientation-only ~1.06-1.18 m/s: these are the motion-distribution-prior level (a prior over speed and direction given attitude; speed bias -0.56 for imu_only, i.e. regresses to small speeds). Two frames cut error by 3x, so ~70% of the velocity information is geometric (parallax) rather than prior. MEASURED. Single image plus IMU gives no gain over IMU only (1.08 vs 1.06): no useful single-frame scale cue (depth/semantic prior) was learned at 25 ep, UNKNOWN whether longer training would find one.
- Gyro derotation matters hugely: raw2 0.581 vs derotated 0.349 (+67%). The explicit rotation compensation is physics the small net cannot learn itself. flow_raw 0.62 likewise.
- IMU adds moderately given images: img_only 0.400 vs all 0.349 (-13% by IMU: gravity/attitude/short-term acceleration cues, direction in camera frame; accelerometer carries no velocity information, no drag).
- Appearance beats an LK flow front-end: flow_derot 0.423 > all 0.349; the CNN extracts more than coarse flow (e.g. confidence, texture, parallax gradients, blur). Sparse tracks MLP worse (0.488) despite 3x params: but it is a crude 16x16-cell encoding, so not a verdict on tracks+IMU.
- Fx removal costs +0.013 (above seed noise): intrinsics matter somewhat; ray coordinates give nothing (0.348).
- A third frame helps modestly: seq3 -0.011 overall, -0.031 on fast (7%), speed bias shrinks (-0.032 -> -0.009), at +1 frame of context and ~1.5x input-prep and conv fuel. Single seed for seq3, so the overall -0.011 is only ~3 sigma_seed; the fast-subset gain is likelier real. HYPOTHESIS: longer windows (parallax accumulation) are the principal untapped information; the net uses a 1-frame gap pair (gap 1 at 20 Hz?) while the filter has 6 clones.
Speed-only + geometric direction (NEW EXPERIMENT, MEASURED, out/diag.json 'geodir'): direction from a classical geometric estimate (flow-derived translation direction) has 35.5 deg mean error vs net 9.4 deg; hybrids (net speed + geometric direction) err 0.90 vs net 0.326. Gating by median parallax >5/10/20 px only ties the net (0.326/0.331/0.359) at 7/20/39% coverage. Using the true direction with net speed gives 0.212, true speed with geometric direction 0.863. Conclusion: full-vector regression strongly beats a geometric direction at 64x48 / 1-frame gap, because typical median parallax is ~4 px. Direction from geometry only wins where parallax is large (not yet measured to beat the net there: gate5 equals net, no gain). The deployed MSCKF already extracts geometric direction over long baselines, so the net's role is the metric scale and the short-time prior.
Caveat for all: short recipe, 1-2 seeds, val only on head K and anchors with pos>=1; in-filter effects not tested (UNKNOWN).

### Direct-C vs D-then-distill vs modular (HYPOTHESIS, informed by the measurements)
- Under single-best-point scoring, the highest-value target region is A/near-A (<=1-10 M fuel at ~0.5 m): d gain/d ln(ATE) -3.9..-4.6 vs fuel -1.15, so ATE improvements are worth ~3.4x their log-fuel. Target C is worth *less* than the current best (8.00 vs 8.23): reaching 0.15 m at 100 M is not a win unless ATE is much lower; only the gain from fuel cuts at constant ATE (e.g. 0.586 m at 1 M = 9.86) or modest ATE gains at <10 M matter.
- Direct training of a large end-to-end model (D, F) is not supported by evidence here (data scale, no GT depth); distillation from a big depth/flow teacher into a <=5 M MAC student is the only route to C-style information at <=10 M fuel. Teachers need re-rendered GT (i5), otherwise the teacher is a pretrained model with an unknown sim gap.
- Modular improvements we can already quantify: (1) multi-frame context for the velocity net (seq3: fast -7% val at ~1.5x conv cost, HYPOTHESIS that longer windows help more); (2) bigger/long-trained nets (X80 -3%, saturating, but cost 2x fuel and later landing); (3) improve derotation/inputs: already used. Resolution (64x48) was not ablated here (UNKNOWN; NEW EXPERIMENT needed: 96x72 and 128x96 inputs, estimated cost 2.25x/4x in conv).
- Smaller nets for lower-fuel targets: A2_kd (68k params) 0.350 vs 0.298 (+17% direct error) for ~35% less compute (EXISTING: only helps at <=3 M budgets).

## 6. Bottom line
Recommend: refine, not replace. The deployed velocity net is calibrated, not overfit, near its information ceiling for 2 frames at 64x48 (data/size saturate at ~3%). The only measured lever beyond that is temporal context (seq3) and, MEASURED, the explicit gyro derotation (+67% error if removed). Single-frame scale/depth was not learned (1.08), so metric-depth architectures C/D need external supervision or teachers and are unlikely to fit <=10 M fuel without distillation experiments; Target D is far out of reach of our data (brief).
